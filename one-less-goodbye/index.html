For Rajesh

One Less Goodbye

Dear Rajesh,

You’re the person I turn to when I feel lost. You help me figure out what to do next, you teach me new things, and you remind me to keep going after my dreams. Even on busy days, I know you think of me.

I know I’ve told you I’d come and then changed my mind. I’ve even told you to find someone else. I can see why it’s hard to trust my words after hearing both things from me. You’ve been waiting to see me, and I’m sorry I’ve made that wait harder.

I’ve made up my mind about what I want. I want to come to India to see you, and I want to marry you. I’m still figuring out when I can travel. I’ll keep you in the loop as I work out the details, and when I have a date I can commit to, I’ll tell you.

We’ve been friends for so long, and there’s something I’ve never said out loud.

♡

I love you, Rajesh.

I’m looking forward to the day I get to say that with you right in front of me.

Love,
Jahnavi
